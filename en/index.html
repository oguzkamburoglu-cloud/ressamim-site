Lessons

The teacher shows, the child draws

Each lesson has a few short steps. The teacher draws the line on screen while explaining it. The child draws the same line with a finger or a stylus and collects stars. The video shows the first lesson of the Little Ones section: the sun.

  • Little Ones (ages 4–6): sun, balloon, apple
  • Pencil: cat, straight lines, circles
  • Watercolor and Color: the first three lessons each

Each section has 50 lessons. The rest open with the family membership.

Studio

Free drawing and coloring pages

A blank page for children who want to keep drawing after the lessons. The teacher suggests a new idea every day.

  • Pencil, eraser and 6 colors are free
  • 3 free coloring pages: apple, fish, butterfly
  • One tap saves the drawing to My drawings

The watercolor brush, all 12 colors and 50 coloring pages come with the family membership.

Game

Mix the colors

The teacher asks for a color: orange, green or purple. The child picks two primary colors and sees the result, learning by playing that red and yellow make orange.

There are 9 games. The other 8 open with the family membership.

Magazine

Meet the artists

Each issue introduces one artist in short, simple words, with a "Did you know?" corner and a task to try in the studio. Issue 1 is Vincent van Gogh and his swirly sky.

The first of 50 issues is free, the rest come with the family membership.
