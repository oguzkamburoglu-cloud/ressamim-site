How it works

Just like a real teacher

Every lesson has a few short steps. Each step has the same three parts.

  1. The teacher shows

    Draws the line slowly on screen and explains it out loud.

  2. A small tip

    A short, simple technique tip, like "Look, my hand goes round."

  3. The child's turn

    The child draws the same line with a finger or a stylus and collects stars.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Write to us. destek@ressamim.com

What ages is it for?

Ages 4–12. The app starts at the child's age: the Little Ones section for ages 4–6, and Pencil, Watercolor and Color for older children.

Do I need to enter payment details for the free part?

No. Download the app and use the free parts right away. The membership is optional.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets. Children can draw with a finger or a stylus.

Does it need the internet?

The teacher's voice is downloaded the first time and saved on the device. After that it also works offline.

Which languages are supported?

11 languages: Turkish, English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, Bengali, French, Russian, Portuguese and Urdu. The app follows the phone's language, and you can change it in the parents' area.

How do I cancel the membership?

At any time, in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
